# Amelioras — Current experience and proposed offerings

## Available on this website

The browser-local planner turns one everyday goal into a short, explained list of planning ideas. It responds to a household-space constraint and the preferred starting approach. Choices are not saved or submitted, and the output is not a diagnosis, property assessment, or validated wellbeing score.

Existing email links allow a visitor to ask about the concept or a possible partnership. The planner does not automatically send its contents.

## Intended pathways

The broader concept includes:

1. Small, practical adjustments to everyday arrangements and routines.
2. Carefully chosen living kits, subject to sourcing, testing, availability, and accurate product information.
3. Practical service coordination, subject to an established provider network, scope, and terms.
4. Clearly scoped community and institutional pilots, shaped with participants and appropriate permission.

These proposed pathways are not an active product catalog, booked home service, verified provider network, or promised funding program. No health, stress, sleep, or financial outcome is established by the illustrative examples.
