# Ameliora — Core Offerings & System Architecture

## The Problem Ameliora Solves
Millions of individuals and families live in spaces and daily circumstances that feel stressful, depleted, and inadequate. However, their options in the marketplace are deeply polarized:
- **Luxury Interior Remodelers & Designers**: Exclusively serve affluent homeowners with \$25,000+ budgets.
- **Charity & Emergency Shelter Relief**: Focused strictly on crisis stabilization (homelessness, acute disaster relief), with no capacity for everyday living improvements.
- **Generic Consumer Retailers**: Sell fragmented decor without systemic assessment of sleep, lighting, air quality, acoustic friction, or domestic routines.

**Ameliora occupies the practical middle**: affordable, systematic, evidence-informed living upgrades designed for people who simply want to live better today.

---

## 1. The Living Assessment Engine

The starting point for every household is the **Living Assessment**—a structured, dignity-centered diagnostic completed in 10–15 minutes on mobile or assisted by a community partner representative.

### Diagnostic Dimensions
1. **Sleep Architecture**: Ambient light intrusion, mattress ergonomics, room temperature fluctuations, bed-partner sleep disruptions, and evening wind-down cues.
2. **Environmental Comfort**: Acoustic friction (neighbor noise, traffic, appliance hum), ambient air quality (particulates, ventilation, odor, humidity), and daylight access.
3. **Daily Routine & Friction Mapping**: Morning congestion, laundry/cooking bottlenecks, workspace ergonomics, charge-station clutter, and evening reset rituals.
4. **Physical Organization & Function**: Micro-storage, entryway sorting, food-prep flow, and accessibility/mobility considerations.
5. **Dignity & Emotional Resonance**: Subjective feelings of pride, privacy, safety, calm, and personal agency within the space.
6. **Household Financial Constraints**: Monthly disposable income, rent vs. own status, lease restrictions on physical alterations, and priority spending thresholds.

### The Living Score & Dynamic Roadmap
The assessment feeds an AI-assisted evaluation engine (aligned with Joseph Jilovec's applied data and automation systems) that computes:
- An overarching **Living Score (0–100)** with sub-scores across physical comfort, rest quality, and domestic flow.
- A **Prioritized Upgrade Roadmap** categorized by financial investment, installation complexity, and time-to-impact.

## 2. Personalized Upgrade Pathways

Upgrades are organized into three distinct, non-intimidating tiers:

### Tier I: Zero-to-Low Cost Friction Reducers (Under \$50)
- Immediate layout adjustments (furniture repositioning for natural light, sightline simplification).
- Lighting temperature alignment (swapping harsh cool bulbs for 2700K circadian bulbs in resting areas).
- Noise masking strategies and textile placement for acoustic dampening.
- Micro-habit architecture (single-touch entryway drops, visual clutter screens).

### Tier II: Targeted Product Bundles & Living Kits (\$50 – \$350)
- **The Restful Sanctuary Kit**: Blackout magnetic shades, dual-density acoustic draft guards, HEPA room purifier, and ergonomic support pillows.
- **The Morning Flow Kit**: High-efficiency organization caddies, visual daily anchor boards, task lighting, and cable consolidation conduits.
- **The Clean Air & Calm Kit**: Compact air-quality monitor, moisture-wicking bedding components, and low-VOC surface enhancers.

### Tier III: Coordinated Vetted Services (\$150+)
- Vetted handyperson services for non-structural wall anchoring, shelving, and door alignment.
- Professional decluttering and spatial reset teams who respect lived experience and tenant autonomy.
- Minor weatherization, draft sealing, and window insulation.

## 3. Delivery, Tracking & Felt Outcomes

Ameliora does not hand users a list of chores and leave. The digital companion platform provides:
- **Step-by-Step Guidance**: Step-by-step modular tasks with video walkthroughs and landlord-safe modification checks.
- **Felt Outcome Check-Ins**: Recurring weekly pulse checks measuring self-reported morning energy, perceived stress reduction, hours of restful sleep, and domestic satisfaction.
- **Continuous Adaptation**: Roadmaps recalibrate as household needs shift (e.g., child starting school, remote work transition, changing seasons).
