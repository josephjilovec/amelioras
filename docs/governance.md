# Ameliora — Governance, Transparency & Board Structure

## Operating Philosophy
Ameliora rejects the false dichotomy between aggressive commercial execution and mission-bound stewardship. We operate as a standard for-profit entity, but our governance is architected to protect community trust, ensure accountability to constituents, and prevent unilateral value extraction.

---

## 1. Decentralized Board of Directors

The governing board is intentionally decentralized to represent all primary stakeholders in our shared-prosperity ecosystem. No single founder or capital provider holds unchecked voting authority.

### Board Composition (5–7 Seats)
1. **Founder & Operations Seat**: Responsible for day-to-day execution, technical roadmaps, and business performance.
2. **Community Representative Seat (Elected / Rotational)**: Held by an active program participant or constituent from an enrolled community, ensuring user lived experience guides policy.
3. **Nonprofit Partner Seat**: Appointed from collaborating community-service organizations, monitoring alignment with vulnerable populations.
4. **Faith & Civic Partner Seat**: Appointed from participating regional churches or interfaith coalitions engaged in living conditions improvement.
5. **Technical & Environmental Health Specialist**: Expert in residential air quality, ergonomics, building science, or preventive public health.
6. **Financial Transparency & Ethics Auditor**: Independent CPA or legal trustee responsible for certifying the Public Balance Sheet.

### Reserved Governance Powers
- **Reinvestment Percentage**: Board approval is mandatory for any alteration to the minimum 60% surplus reinvestment benchmark.
- **Executive Compensation Caps**: Founder and executive salaries are capped at a reasonable multiple of the median company wage, preventing executive inflation.
- **Data Privacy & Monetization Veto**: The Community and Ethics seats possess absolute veto power over any third-party data sharing or research product initiatives.

---

## 2. Public-Facing Balance Sheet & Financial Auditing

Ameliora believes transparency is the ultimate antidote to cynical corporate marketing.

### Disclosure Cadence & Scope
- **Quarterly Ledger Publication**: Published openly at `amelioras.com/transparency`.
- **Open-Book Unit Economics**: Discloses the exact manufacturing cost, shipping overhead, labor cost, and retail price of every product kit and service bundle.
- **Reinvestment Tracking**: Detailed ledger entries showing exact fund transfers into partner community projects, client subsidies, and emergency upgrade reserves.

---

## 3. Inclusion & Cultural Integrity

Inclusivity and diversity are operating requirements across our entire delivery pipeline:
- **Tenant-First Modification Safety**: Products and instructions are universally engineered for renters and low-security leases, guaranteeing no security deposit forfeiture.
- **Multi-Lingual & Multi-Literacy Access**: Assessments and guides are available in English, Spanish, and high-readability visual formats.
- **Fair-Wage Supplier Verification**: All service partners, cleaners, and tradespeople must sign our fair-wage code of conduct to be listed in the Ameliora network.
