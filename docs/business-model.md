# Amelioras — Public business and reinvestment overview

Amelioras is being developed as a for-profit living-improvement concept. The intended offer combines practical household planning with possible future products, coordination services, and institutional or community partnerships.

## Reinvestment intent

The planning target is to direct at least 60% of net operating surplus toward community and partnership benefit. This is a proposed policy target, not a statement of historical revenue, distributed funds, or audited performance.

A dated public balance sheet and reinvestment ledger are intended future reporting tools. The current website does not publish an operating financial report or independent audit.

## Current public experience

The website implements an illustrative household planner and existing email contact routes. It does not implement subscriptions, institutional contracting, a partner funding account, telemetry, or a financial reporting platform.

The complete prior working model was preserved unchanged in the private Venture Studio document repository before this summary replaced it. Detailed operating assumptions remain private. Previous public versions may remain in Git history.
