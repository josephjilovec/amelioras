# Ameliora — Business Model & Shared Prosperity

## Overview
Ameliora is a for-profit company by design. This structure allows the organization to compete for municipal, housing authority, employer, and institutional contracts, generate dependable operating revenue, invest in top-tier technology and talent, and scale without dependency on philanthropic grant cycles.

However, Ameliora operates under a strict **shared-prosperity charter**: the intent of the enterprise is not founder or shareholder extraction, but rather the continuous, measurable improvement of human living conditions. The majority of operating surplus is systematically reinvested into product improvements, community subsidies, partnership co-delivery, and accessible living upgrades.

---

## Primary Revenue Streams

### 1. Direct Household Memberships & Living Plans
- Tiered access to the AI-assisted Living Assessment engine, seasonal routine optimization, continuous comfort telemetry, and ongoing roadmaps.
- Low-barrier monthly or annual subscriptions with sliding-scale tiers for low-income households subsidized through community reinvestment.

### 2. Curated Product Kits & Facilitated Upgrades
- Sourced and vetted friction-reduction bundles (acoustic dampening kits, circadian lighting adjustments, HEPA/air-quality upgrades, ergonomic sleep enhancements, micro-organization systems).
- Direct product margins and negotiated manufacturer volume discounts passed through to users to preserve affordability.

### 3. Vetted Service Facilitation & Coordination
- Premium coordination fees for scheduling and supervising vetted local service providers (deep organization, ergonomic adjustments, minor home efficiency tune-ups, accessibility modifications).
- Transparent service margins with guaranteed fair wages for all partner tradespeople.

### 4. Institutional, Municipal & Employer Contracts
- Enterprise contracts with municipal housing authorities, managed property portfolios, university student housing, and regional employers aiming to reduce domestic friction, absenteeism, and sleep deprivation among their workforce.
- Programmatic service contracts funded through community development block grants (CDBG) and preventative health allocations.

### 5. Anonymized Environmental Outcomes & Research Reporting
- Longitudinal, privacy-preserving reporting on living conditions, comfort indices, and community friction reduction.
- Purchased by housing policymakers, urban planners, public health researchers, and institutional partners evaluating non-clinical determinants of wellbeing.

## The Public Balance Sheet

Ameliora publishes a quarterly **Public Balance Sheet and Reinvestment Ledger** accessible directly on the web. This report itemizes:
1. Every dollar received by channel (Individual, Contract, Products, Research).
2. Direct cost of goods, living kit manufacturing, and fair-wage labor disbursements.
3. Operating overhead (infrastructure, security, compliance, personnel).
4. Reinvestment allocations (subsidized household roadmaps, partner grant disbursements).
5. Retained reserve balances.

By establishing absolute transparency, Ameliora removes the cynicism surrounding traditional corporate social responsibility and demonstrates that commercial rigor can exist in complete service of human flourishing.
